# Prompts des illustrations — Priya Padma

Génération avec l’outil intégré **imagegen**, six appels distincts. Chaque prompt envoyé est constitué du préambule commun ci-dessous, de deux sauts de ligne, puis de `Primary scene request: ` suivi du texte de la scène. Les PNG finaux sont conservés sans retouche dans `images/`.

## Préambule commun

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: full-screen illustration for a French literary book-fair slideshow for PRIYA PADMA, Sens unique, by Lauise Warinda.
Create an original, beautifully hand-painted watercolor travel-journal illustration, landscape 16:9 composition, ideally 1920x1080 or larger. Fine cold-pressed ivory paper grain, translucent washes and pigment blooms, selective fine graphite marks, sophisticated literary illustration with real architectural observation. Cohesive restrained palette of warm ivory, muted sage, indigo shadows, ochre and terracotta, warm dawn light. Delicate painterly edges, ample airy atmosphere, richly observed focal detail. NO photorealism, NO 3D, NO cartoon, NO printed text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, captions, watermark or page border. The image itself has no typography; typography will be overlaid separately. Compose the important subject in the right half with atmospheric quieter washes in the left third. Keep meaningful subject detail away from the outer 8 percent. Scene is evocative fictional artwork, not an archaeological reconstruction. No invented religious symbols, no glowing runes, no exaggerated fantasy temples.
```

## 01-barabar-aube.png

```text
Primary scene request: Wide view of Barabar's low, rounded, weathered granite outcrop in Bihar at dawn. An immense horizontal smooth whale-back boulder in an open rocky landscape with sparse vegetation. In the right half of the rock there is a small rock-cut doorway and a delicately carved horseshoe arch reminiscent of Lomas Rishi; keep the real modest scale of the human-sized opening against the boulder, no grand columns or temple tower. Pale golden light catches the granite; distant gently layered hills. No people. Mood of invitation, hidden history and quiet anticipation. Do not depict a book or book cover.
```

## 02-geneve.png

```text
Primary scene request: A young woman around twenty seen strictly from behind, an understated dark indigo coat, her face and identifiable physical details entirely hidden, stands slightly right of center on the shore promenade of Lake Geneva. She is small enough to leave most of the painting to the water and sky. Recognizable Geneva lakeside atmosphere with a subtle thin Jet d'Eau fountain far in the distance, the lake, a few distant city roofs and soft mountain forms. Early morning, gently rippling pale water and indigo reflections. Quiet introspection and the beginning of an unknown journey. Avoid any visible supernatural effect. No readable signs.
```

## 03-deux-mondes.png

```text
Primary scene request: A poetic SINGLE continuous watercolor travel-journal spread WITHOUT a seam or drawn map. The cold blue water and discreet roofline of Geneva on the left dissolve through translucent washes into the warm everyday urban residential streetscape of Faridabad, northern India, in the right half: modest flat-roof plaster homes, low balconies, terracotta and pale ochre walls, overhead lines suggested delicately, a leafy tree. Respect ordinary contemporary Faridabad architecture, no Taj Mahal, no palace, no domes or stereotyped tourist landmarks. A soft swath of warm light connects the two places. No people necessary, no transportation, no flags, no letters, no route labels.
```

## 04-mallette.png

```text
Primary scene request: Quiet domestic still life inside an unassuming old family home in northern India. A modest closed, worn brown leather briefcase with small brass clasps, half hidden in the shadows beneath a wooden console or beside an old cupboard, is the focal point in the lower right middle of the composition. Indirect warm light from a window falls across part of its surface and the plaster wall. Subtle aged wood, pale stone floor, translucent indigo shadows. The briefcase is fully closed; its contents remain unseen. No treasure chest, no keys scattered around, no documents, letters, photographs, magic or visible person. A sense of memory and a secret waiting to be discovered.
```

## 05-barabar-interieur.png

```text
Primary scene request: Interior inspired by the actual Sudama cave at Barabar: a simple empty rock-cut granite hall with a barrel-vaulted curved ceiling, smooth continuous stone walls, a flat floor, and a small austere rectangular opening to bright daylight on the far wall in the right half. Grey granite softly reflects daylight in subtle watercolor washes; warm light rests on the floor, indigo deepens the curved corners. Emphasize the beautifully spare, monolithic, human-scale geometry and calm material surfaces. No columns, statues, furnished objects, torches, stalactites, brick masonry, decorative carvings or enormous cathedral scale. No people. A faithful architectural atmosphere translated into delicate watercolor, not a photograph.
```

## 06-seuil.png

```text
Primary scene request: Symbolic literary illustration rather than a literal scene from the book: a small young woman seen strictly from behind as an anonymous indigo silhouette, with no visible face, stands in the right half near a simple opening between dark stone surfaces. Beyond is a gentle luminous field of pale warm gold and washed-out blue, with subtle translucent watercolor reflections suggesting uncertainty and two worlds. Her stance is still and contemplative. Maintain human scale, ambiguous natural light and tactile paper. No supernatural power is depicted literally: no beams from hands, no portal ring, no runes, no extra figures or monsters. The contrast between a cool interior and a luminous threshold carries the emotion.
```
